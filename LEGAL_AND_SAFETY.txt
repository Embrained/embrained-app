SECTION 2: PHYSICAL SAFETY & BATTERY HANDLING GUIDELINES
========================================================================

The Plexus robot platform involves electronics, motors, and lithium-ion
batteries. All users MUST read and follow these safety guidelines before
operating the hardware.

1. Battery Policy ("BYOB" — Bring Your Own Batteries)

To comply with shipping regulations, the Plexus Head and Chassis ship
without batteries. Users assume responsibility for battery integration
and must supply their own:

  - 2x 18650 Lithium-Ion cells (recommended: Samsung 25R or LG HG2
    high-discharge cells)
  - A compatible 18650 charger with overcharge protection

2. Polarity & Handling

  - ALWAYS observe correct battery polarity when inserting cells.
    Reversed polarity can cause permanent damage or fire.
  - Never use damaged, dented, or re-wrapped cells.
  - Do not short-circuit the battery terminals.
  - Do not expose batteries to extreme heat or open flame.

3. Hardware Safeguards

The Plexus PCB includes secondary safety mechanisms:

  - P-Channel MOSFET for reverse polarity protection
  - PTC Polyfuse to prevent motor stall current from damaging the
    logic board

These are SECONDARY safeguards. The PRIMARY safety mechanism is strict
user compliance with these handling instructions.

4. Operating Environment

  - Operate the robot on flat, indoor surfaces only.
  - Do not operate near stairs, ledges, water, or pets without
    supervision.
  - The robot communicates over WiFi — ensure a stable local network
    connection before enabling autonomous navigation.
  - Never leave the robot running unattended during autonomous mode.

5. Electrical Safety

  - Do not modify the Plexus PCB unless you are qualified to work
    with electronics.
  - Disconnect batteries before performing any hardware maintenance.
  - Use only the recommended power supply voltages specified in the
    hardware documentation.
